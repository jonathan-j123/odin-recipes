Veggie Pizza

A colorful and healthy pizza loaded with fresh vegetables and a delicious tomato sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 pizza dough
  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce
  • 1 cup mozzarella cheese, shredded
  • 1/2 cup bell peppers, sliced
  • 1/2 cup mushrooms, sliced
  • 1/4 cup red onions, sliced
  • 1/4 cup black olives, sliced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried basil

Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 475°F.
  2. Roll out the pizza dough on a floured surface.
  3. Spread the tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  4. Sprinkle the mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
  5. Add the sliced vegetables on top of the cheese.
  6. Sprinkle with dried oregano and basil.
  7. Bake for 12-15 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbly.
  8. Remove from oven, slice, and serve hot.
