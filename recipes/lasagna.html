Lasagna

A hearty and delicious Italian classic, perfect for feeding a crowd.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese, shredded
  • 1/4 cup parmesan cheese, grated

Steps:

  1. Cook the lasagna noodles according to package instructions.
  2. In a large skillet, brown the ground beef and drain excess fat.
  3. Add the onion and garlic, cooking until softened.
  4. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, basil, and oregano.
  5. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  6. In a separate bowl, mix the ricotta cheese with 1 cup of mozzarella cheese.
  7. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
  8. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in the bottom of a 9x13 inch baking dish.
  9. Layer half of the noodles, followed by half of the cheese mixture, and half of the meat sauce.
  10. Repeat the layers once more.
  11. Sprinkle the remaining mozzarella and parmesan cheeses on top.
  12. Bake for 25-30 minutes until bubbly and golden brown.
