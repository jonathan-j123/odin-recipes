Fried Chicken

Deliciously crispy and golden brown, this fried chicken is a classic comfort food that's perfect for any occasion.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken pieces
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp paprika
  • 1/4 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/4 tsp onion powder
  • 1 cup vegetable oil

Steps:

  1. Preheat the oil to 375°F.
  2. Dredge the chicken pieces in the flour mixture.
  3. Deep fry the chicken until golden brown and cooked through.
  4. drain on paper towels.
